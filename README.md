Brand Introduction
Dawn Cold Drink is a beverage brand concept built around freshness, energy, flavour, and the simple pleasure of enjoying a refreshing drink. Its digital identity is designed to feel vibrant, welcoming, and memorable.

The Dawn website brings this identity to life through a modern visual experience that introduces the brand, presents its products, shares its story, and gives visitors a simple way to get in touch. Every section is designed to support a consistent brand presence while keeping the browsing experience clear and intuitive.

The idea behind Dawn is simple: make every moment feel refreshing.
Project Overview
Dawn Cold Drink is a custom-developed, responsive WordPress theme created to showcase a beverage brand through an engaging and user-friendly website. It combines WordPress theme development with core frontend technologies to deliver a lightweight experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

The website brings together brand storytelling, product discovery, visual presentation, and customer enquiries in one cohesive digital experience.

Development Approach
The website is developed as a custom WordPress theme rather than as a separate frontend application. PHP is used for WordPress theme templates and integration, while HTML5 and CSS3 define the content structure and visual presentation. Vanilla JavaScript supports interactive elements such as navigation and interface behaviour.

This approach keeps the project focused on core web technologies and WordPress while avoiding the overhead of an additional frontend framework.
Design Direction
The visual direction of Dawn Cold Drink is centred on a fresh, energetic, and contemporary beverage-brand identity. Product-focused imagery, clear content sections, responsive layouts, and intuitive navigation work together to create a cohesive website experience.

The design prioritises:
- Strong first impressions through the homepage hero area.
- Clear presentation of products and brand information.
- Consistent visual styling across all sections.
- Readable content and straightforward navigation.
- Adaptable layouts for different screen sizes.
Responsive Experience
The theme is designed to adapt to common desktop, tablet, and smartphone screen sizes. Its responsive layout and mobile navigation help maintain usability across devices, allowing visitors to explore the brand and its sections without depending on a desktop-sized display.
